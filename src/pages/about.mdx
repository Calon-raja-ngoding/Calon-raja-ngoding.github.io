## My Journey

My journey into programming began during my school years, where I first became
interested in how software and technology work.

Since then, I've continued exploring programming through learning,
experimentation, and hands-on projects. Along the way, I've gained an interest
in different areas of technology and discovered that there's always something
new to understand and explore.

For me, programming is not just about writing code. It's also about developing
logical thinking, approaching challenges, and finding ways to solve problems.

## Areas of Interest

I'm interested in exploring several areas of technology, including:

**Web Development** — Creating functional and accessible websites and web

**Database & Data Engineering** — Exploring database systems, data management,

## How I Learn

I enjoy learning through problem-solving. Rather than simply following

wrong, and exploring different ways to approach a problem.

I believe that making mistakes and learning from them are important parts of
developing technical skills.

I'm looking forward to discovering new possibilities and growing into a
